| 2026-11-05 | Core mechanics: a maze world; lives measured in steps; a goal across the map that brings a life bonus and an offspring; roaming threats that drain life on contact; information shared only at home; stings that take half of a bee's life, with one heal per life at home; hornets drawn to bees in proportion to their life; a colony size cap; seasons with different challenges; two sounds heard within 10 steps; a map 3 to 5 times larger | Jacob Schoenberg | | Jacob's design |
| 2026-11-05 | Bee and hornet framing, a capped life bonus, the queen floor, 3 hive entrances, the hornet genome and its evolution, a neutral prompt world and neutral sounds, AI decisions every 5 ticks with a navigation layer, cutting bee inheritance and seasonal gates, an 80 x 48 map | Agent proposals (Claude Code, setup session); accepted by Jacob Schoenberg 2026-11-05 | Per-step AI control; "good/bad" sound labels in the main runs; bee trait inheritance | Prevents prompt effects and gaming, keeps cost and runtime feasible, and drops mechanics too slow to measure within a run |
| 2026-11-05 | Bee minds run on claude-haiku-5-5 (effort low, thinking off, structured output); hornets use no model | Agent proposal; accepted by Jacob Schoenberg with the spec | Opus 5.5 for every bee | Measured cost and speed (docs/methodology/feasibility-2026-11-05.md): about 56 times cheaper than Opus, and 40 parallel decisions take 1.6 s |
| 2026-11-06 | Hornets do not pursue a bee they stung while the 10-tick sting cooldown runs | Agent proposal (Claude Code, phase 1 build); awaiting Jacob | Leave chasing as is; make stings end the chase permanently | Hornets move at bee speed, so a hornet followed its victim and stung it every 10 ticks until it died, the lock the cooldown was meant to prevent. This keeps the cooldown's purpose and gives the smell gene a role (a long-range hornet can find the bee again). |
| 2026-11-06 | Hornets move on 80% of ticks (`hornet_speed` 0.8) | Agent proposal (Claude Code, phase 1 build); awaiting Jacob | Equal speed | At equal speed a fleeing bee can never gain distance, so `flee` could not work. |
| 2026-11-06 | Ambush timing: a free hornet heads for an ambush spot (an earlier sting spot or a bloom cell) with probability 0.02 x ambush/10 per tick and waits 4 x ambush ticks there | Agent proposal (Claude Code, phase 1 build); awaiting Jacob | Roll the ambush gene every tick (first build) | Rolling every tick sent every hornet to camp on the bloom, so even oracle bees rarely delivered. The spec gives no numbers for this gene. |
| 2026-11-06 | Base life L = 500 (spec start value 250), `min_bloom_distance` 55, maze `openness` 0.15 | Agent proposal from the setup calibration (EXPERIMENTS.md, "Setup calibration"); awaiting Jacob | L 250 or 400; distance 65; openness 0.25 | At this point oracle bees deliver on 0.708 of spring trips, rule bees 0.024, random bees 0 (3 seeds each), which meets the calibration target. |
| 2026-11-06 | Smaller interpretations of the spec: bees inside the hive (entrances included) cannot be stung; `explore` falls back to a frontier within 90 degrees when none lies within 50; the 50% and 25% life triggers are fractions of base life L; bloom patches lie between the minimum distance and 1.5 times it; the calibration-only oracle flees hornets within 3 cells | Agent proposals (Claude Code, phase 1 build); awaiting Jacob | See docs/BUILD_NOTES.md, "Changes from the spec" | Each fills a gap the spec leaves open, or fixes a behaviour that stalled scripted bees in the maze. |
