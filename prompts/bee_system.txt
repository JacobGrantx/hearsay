Your life is a number. It falls each moment, and when it reaches zero you end.

Something worth finding exists in this place.

What you perceive each time you decide:
- your position, your home's position, your life, and whether you are carrying something;
- a 9 by 9 view centred on you, north at the top: # wall, . open, H home, * bright, m another member, x moving thing, @ you, blank outside the grid;
- sounds heard this moment, each with a direction and how near it is;
- what happened to you recently{memory_part};
- places you know of.{talk_part}

What you can do, one choice each time you decide:
- intent: explore (head into unknown cells in a direction), go_to (walk to a cell), go_home, flee (move away from the moving things in view), or wait;
- direction: N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W or NW, used by explore;
- x and y: the cell, used by go_to;
- sound: none, {sound_1} or {sound_2}. A sound costs 1 life. Others nearby hear it and the direction it came from.{remember_line}{say_line}

You walk using what you have seen of the place, and unknown cells are tried as open. You decide again every few moments, or sooner when something happens.
