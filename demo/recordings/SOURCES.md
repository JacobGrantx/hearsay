## Narration (as spoken)

- `01_open`: Hearsay asks one question of digital life: can knowledge outlive the one who found it? It is a colony of language-model agents in a maze. They find things, can share them only at home, and die.
- `02_world`: Each bee is a Claude Haiku 5.5 mind. It sees only nearby cells, keeps its own memories, and can talk only at home. Hornets evolve by selection and mutation. Seasons move the bloom. The bees are never told what the hornets or the bloom are; they get only plain words like moving thing and bright cell.
- `03_first_sight`: Tick 34. Bee 5 sees a moving thing for the first time, and flees. It has never been stung, so this caution was not learned. It came from the model's prior, the word flee in its prompt, or both.
- `04_stung`: Bee 1 picks up bloom at tick 100. A hornet stings it twice. It carries the bloom home anyway, and arrives with 23 life left.
- `05_warns`: At home it tells newborn bee 6: bright cells there are dangerous. No prompt contains the word danger.
- `06_e5_finder`: Experiment five: a smaller world, with the bloom in reach. Bee 4 finds it at tick 60 and delivers at tick 166, and bee 6 hatches.
- `07_e5_tells`: At home, bee 4 says: back home carrying a bright thing from around 36, 3, found in the east. Members should know it is there.
- `08_e5_hears`: Bee 6, six ticks old, writes: heard the valuable thing was found near 37, 3, in the east. Explore east next.
- `09_e5_delivers`: It reaches the bloom at tick 224 and delivers at tick 330. Talk carried a place, in one seed of five. The bar we set in advance was two.
- `10_summer`: Summer. The bloom has moved. Bee 1 goes back to spring's old place, and is stung twice there. Fleeing home, it finds the summer bloom and delivers.
- `11_winter`: Winter. Nothing blooms. Bees run out of life, and the colony falls from 7 to 4.
- `12_hypotheses`: We registered every hypothesis in advance. None was supported. Stings rose in year two even with hornets that could not evolve. No bee lived through both springs. And the bees made 3 sounds in 6,610 decisions.
- `13_chart_e1`: With memory and talk, bees made 1.8 deliveries in two years, the same as scripted rule bees.
- `14_chart_caution`: At a first sight of a moving thing, bees fled or went home 0.857 of the time, and 1.000 of the time with no memory at all, against 0.110 to 0.282 at routine decisions.
- `15_chart_e5`: In experiment five, talk did not pay: 5.2 deliveries a year with talk, 8.2 without. And colonies that could talk stayed home more.
- `16_brief`: How Hearsay meets the brief. Technical depth: evolving hornet genomes, memory with recall, a navigation layer, a leak-tested perception boundary, and byte-identical replay. Originality: culture and memory in a grounded, mortal colony, not a text-only chat. Behaviour and experiments: pre-registered hypotheses, ablations and baselines, and negative results reported as such. Engineering: make demo replays a logged run with no API key, with seeds and tests. Tool use: every agent session's trace is in the repository, and the AI disclosure says who did what.
- `17_end`: A second round, with abstract words that carry no real-world meaning, ran on the morning of the deadline. Its results are in the read-me. Every frame here is a logged run. Replay it with make demo.
