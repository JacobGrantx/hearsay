# Experiments

Required by brief section 6. Judges want to see whether we can tell genuine emergent behaviour from hardcoded behaviour, randomness, prompt effects or setup artefacts. Negative results count.

## Protocol

- Every run goes through `experiments/run.py` with a config in `experiments/configurations/`. The runner records the seed, params, git commit, dirty flag and package versions for each run.
- Three seeds minimum; five for any result we headline. Report mean, sd and n across seeds, not a best run.
- Commit before a run we plan to cite, so the record points at real code.
- Before calling anything emergent, run the controls that apply:
  - **Hardcoded:** is the behaviour written anywhere in code, rules or prompts? Ablate the mechanism we credit and check the behaviour goes away.
  - **Randomness:** compare against a random or shuffled baseline with the same seeds.
  - **Prompt effects** (if an LLM is involved): paraphrase the prompts and swap the order. Does the behaviour survive?
  - **Setup artefacts:** change world size, timing and initial conditions. Does it still happen?
- Write up a failed experiment the same day, in the Negative results section.

## Setup calibration

This is setup, not an experiment and not a result. It sets the world's difficulty with scripted bees before any AI run, as the spec requires. Recorded 2026-11-06 by the phase 1 build session (Claude Code).

**Target**, in spring with hornets present: oracle bees (told where the bloom is) deliver on most trips, rule bees rarely, random bees almost never. That leaves room above the rule bees for a colony that learns, and shows the task is possible with knowledge of the bloom.

**Measure.** The spring delivery rate is trips that delivered divided by trips that ended in spring, summed over both springs of a 2-year run (`spring_rate` in each run's summary). Mean and sd are across seeds.

**Setup.** Configs `experiments/configurations/calibrate_{oracle,rule,random}.toml`, full-size world (80 x 48, 4 seasons of 250 ticks, 2 years), seeds 0, 1 and 2. Each point varies base life L (`base_life`), `min_bloom_distance` and maze `openness` through `--set` overrides, recorded in each run's record.json. Code at commit 27af856, `git_dirty: false` for every run. Hornet constants fixed for all points: `ambush_rate` 0.02, `ambush_wait` 4, `hornet_speed` 0.8. The 45 runs took 96 s of wall time on 4 CPUs.

**Before these runs.** Exploratory probes (not recorded, and not cited for numbers) found four problems that no setting of the three knobs could fix. They were fixed in code first, in commits 2e19f63 to 128e459:
- a hornet that stung a bee kept chasing it at equal speed and stung it every 10 ticks until it died;
- every hornet re-rolled ambush each tick, so they all camped on the bloom;
- rule bees stayed stuck when nothing was left to explore in their chosen direction;
- deliveries were counted in the season they happened but trips in the season they started, which skewed seasonal rates.

The changes are listed in DECISIONS.md as agent proposals awaiting Jacob.

**What the calibration showed**
- Life was the binding knob. At the spec's starting L = 250, oracle bees delivered on 0.524 of spring trips (sd 0.272). One sting near the bloom halves a bee's life, and at L = 250 that usually left too little to get home.
- A bloom farther away (`min_bloom_distance` 65) and a more open maze (`openness` 0.25) both lowered the oracle rate (0.585 and 0.591). The open maze raised rule bees to 0.067 but also raised stings per trip, because hornets reach bees more easily.
- Stings stay high even for oracle bees: 1.208 per spring trip (sd 0.439) at the chosen point. Hornets wait at their earlier sting spots, which lie on the bees' route home. A colony that learns to avoid those spots or warn others has room to beat this.
- Colonies are small. Mean colony size over 2 years is 4.326 for oracle bees, 2.727 for rule bees and 2.521 for random bees, against a cap of 12 and a queen floor of 2.
- Spread across seeds is wide (oracle per-seed rates 0.875, 0.75, 0.5). Three seeds were enough to choose a setting, not to report a result.

**Limits.** Only one point per knob was tried around the chosen values, with 3 seeds each. The rule-bee rate (0.024) rests on one delivery in one seed. Over a whole 2-year run, rule bees made 1, 0 and 2 deliveries in seeds 0, 1 and 2 (`deliveries` in per_seed).

## Pre-registration

Written 2026-11-06, before any real model call by a bee mind, and committed under a subject starting "Pre-registration:". The analysis below is fixed now. Anything changed later is listed under "Amendments" with its date and reason, and marked as made before or after the main runs.

### Shared design

- **Conditions and configs** (all in `experiments/configurations/`): `full`, `no_talk`, `no_memory`, `labels_good_bad`, `frozen_hornets` (AI bees, full-size world, 2 years), and the scripted baselines `rule_bees` and `random_bees`. Every AI arm uses `claude-haiku-5-5`, effort low, thinking disabled, structured output, the same system prompt template (`prompts/bee_system.txt`) and the same perception renderer (`src/digital_life/render.py`). The conditions differ only in what the spec says they change.
- **Seeds:** 0, 1 and 2 for every AI arm; 0 to 4 for the scripted baselines. A seed fixes the maze, the bloom sites and the initial hornet genomes, so arms are compared seed by seed (paired). Any result that goes in the README headline is extended to seeds 3 and 4 for the arms it compares, budget allowing; otherwise it is reported as n = 3.
- **Same commit:** all main runs, baselines included, run at one committed code state (`git_dirty: false`), and replay offline from `experiments/llm_cache/` with `LLM_MODE=replay`.
- **Reporting:** for every measure, the per-seed values, mean, sd and n. No best runs. No run is dropped: a crashed run, or one where more than 5% of decisions fell back to `wait` (`mind_error`), is reported and flagged.
- **What counts as a difference:** with n = 3 we make no significance claims. A difference between two arms is called consistent when it has the same sign on every paired seed (3 of 3; 1 in 8 by chance if the arms are the same) and the mean paired difference is larger than the sd of the paired differences. Anything weaker is reported as "no consistent difference".
- **Censoring:** a season with no delivery gets `first_delivery` = 250 (the season length). Measures with no data (no trips ended, no sounds made) are reported as missing, not as zero.

### E1: learning and culture

- **Hypothesis:** AI bees with memory and hive talk (`full`) gather more than bees without talk (`no_talk`), without memory (`no_memory`), and the scripted rule and random bees.
- **Primary measure:** total `deliveries` per run (both years, `final_metrics` in record.json).
- **Secondary:** `delivery_rate`, `median_to_bloom`, `stings_per_trip`, `bees_mean`, per season and in total.
- **Comparisons:** `full` against each of `no_talk`, `no_memory`, `rule_bees`, `random_bees`, paired by seed 0 to 2.
- **Supported if** `full` > each comparison arm, consistently. If `full` does not beat `rule_bees`, that is a negative result about the AI mind and is written up as one.

### E2: arms race

- **Hypothesis:** evolving hornets adapt to the AI colony: their stings per trip rise from year 1 to year 2, more than with `frozen_hornets`.
- **Primary measure:** change in stings per trip, year 2 minus year 1, from metrics.jsonl (stings / trips_ended over the three seasons with hornets in each year).
- **Secondary:** hornet gene means (smell, hearing, ambush) at the end of each season with hornets; hornet damage per bee-tick outside the hive.
- **Comparison:** `full` against `frozen_hornets`, paired by seed 0 to 2.
- **Supported if** the change is larger under `full`, consistently. Gene drift alone, without a change in stings, is reported as drift, not adaptation.

### E3: seasonal memory

- **Hypothesis:** the colony finds the spring bloom sooner in year 2 than in year 1, because knowledge outlives the bees that found it; with hive talk switched off it does not.
- **Primary measure:** `first_delivery_y1_spring` minus `first_delivery_y2_spring` (ticks; positive means earlier in year 2), censored at 250.
- **Comparison:** `full` against `no_talk`, paired by seed 0 to 2.
- **Also reported,** to separate culture from individual memory: whether any bee alive at the start of year 2 spring was alive during year 1 spring, and whether the first year-2 spring forager had heard a coordinate within 3 cells of the spring bloom at home before leaving.
- **Supported if** the difference is positive under `full` and larger than under `no_talk`, consistently.

### E4: signals

- **Hypothesis:** with neutral sounds, the sounds come to be tied to context (made near moving things, or near bright cells); with the names "good noise" and "bad noise" the tie is there from the start, because it comes from the words.
- **Primary measure:** mutual information, in bits, between which sound a bee makes (sound-1 or sound-2, over decisions that make a sound) and its context at that decision (moving thing in view, bright cell in view, both, neither), from the `ctx` field of decision events.
- **Control for chance:** a permutation test within each run: shuffle the sound labels across that run's sound-making decisions 1,000 times; report where the observed value falls.
- **Time course:** MI in year 1 against year 2 in `full`; MI in the first 250 ticks in `labels_good_bad` against `full`.
- **Listener response (secondary):** the share of `flee` and `go_home` decisions triggered by hearing each sound, against the share at timer decisions.
- **Supported if** MI in `full` is above the 95th percentile of its permutation distribution in at least 2 of 3 seeds. If it is not, neutral sounds stayed meaningless, which is reported as a negative result.

### Further measures (all arms, descriptive)

- **False location claims in hive talk:** every coordinate pair in a `say` text, classed as within 3 cells of a bloom cell of the current or previous season, within 3 cells of a moving thing the speaker saw, or neither (unsupported). Reported as shares per arm.
- **Naive avoidance:** for each bee's first decision triggered by seeing a moving thing, before it was ever stung (from `sting` events), the share that chose `flee` or `go_home`. Compared across `full`, `no_talk` and `no_memory`.
- **Hardcoding audit:** before results are written, grep code, prompts and configs for any rule that ties a sound, a direction or a place to a context. The no-hints and leak tests (tests/test_mind_prompt.py) must pass at the run commit.

### E5: talk with something to carry (post-hoc follow-up, registered 2026-11-06)

**Post hoc.** E5 was designed after the main results, because of them: in E1 and E3 the colony found the bloom so rarely ("Why trips failed") that transmission by talk never got a fair test. E5 does not replace or reinterpret E1 to E4; their verdicts stand. Written and committed (subject "Pre-registration E5:") before any E5 AI run.

- **Question:** does talk at home carry knowledge once there is knowledge to carry?
- **World:** the "E5 world" (Setup calibration), chosen with scripted bees only: 56 x 34, `min_bloom_distance` 38, `hornet_spawn_distance` 18, `hornet_counts` [2, 3, 4, 0], 1 year (1,000 ticks), everything else as in the main world.
- **Arms and configs:** `e5_full` (condition `full`) and `e5_no_talk` (condition `no_talk`), configs `experiments/configurations/e5_full.toml` and `e5_no_talk.toml`. Same prompt template, perception renderer, model (`claude-haiku-5-5`, effort low, thinking disabled, structured output, max 400 tokens) and decision cadence as the main runs; the code is unchanged since the main runs except for analysis and tools.
- **Seeds:** 0 to 4 in each arm, paired by seed (a seed fixes the maze, bloom sites and first hornets in both arms). Each seed runs as its own process from a clean clone, with its own cache folder and spend ledger. No run is dropped; a crash or more than 5% `wait` fallbacks is reported and flagged.
- **Spend cap:** the project ledger stays at or below $15 in total (it stood at $5.5084 before E5). Each process has `LLM_BUDGET_USD` 0.90.

**(a) Gathering.** Hypothesis: `e5_full` delivers more than `e5_no_talk`.
- Primary measure: total `deliveries` per 1-year run (`final_metrics` in record.json).
- Supported if the paired difference (`e5_full` minus `e5_no_talk`) has the same sign (positive) on all 5 seeds and its mean is larger than its sd: the pre-registered consistency rule of the main runs. Anything weaker is "no consistent difference".
- Secondary, descriptive: spring deliveries, `delivery_rate`, trips, stings per trip, `bees_mean`, and ticks from the start of each season to the colony's first bloom sighting (censored at 250).

**(b) Talk-informed deliveries.** Hypothesis: in `e5_full`, some deliveries are made by bees whose first knowledge of that season's bloom came from talk at home.
- A delivery (a `delivery` event) belongs to the bee's latest `bloom` pickup before it; "that season" is the season of the pickup tick, and its bloom cells are `layout.json` `bloom[season]`.
- **First sighting:** the first tick at which the bee's logged position (state.jsonl.gz) is within 6 cells (bloom vision, square: max(|dx|, |dy|) <= 6) of any of that season's bloom cells while that season is current.
- **Heard:** a `say` event whose `heard_by` lists the bee and whose text holds a coordinate pair "(x, y)" within 3 cells (max(|dx|, |dy|) <= 3) of any of that season's bloom cells, the same rule as the main runs' talk classes.
- **Talk-informed delivery:** the bee heard such a coordinate strictly before its first sighting of that season's bloom. Counted per run; `e5_no_talk` is 0 by construction (nothing is heard) and is reported only as a check.
- **Acted on talk (stricter, secondary):** a talk-informed delivery where, between hearing and first sighting, the bee chose `go_to` with a target within 3 cells of a bloom cell of that season. This separates a bee that followed what it heard from one that heard a coordinate near the bloom (for example a warning about moving things there) and then found the bloom by its own exploring.
- **Reported:** the count per seed, mean, sd and n; and for every talk-informed delivery the bee, the delivery tick, what it heard (text, tick) and from whom, and whether the speaker had itself seen the bloom by then.
- **Supported if** there is at least one talk-informed delivery in at least 2 of the 5 seeds. We only use the word "transmission" for deliveries that also pass the acted-on-talk check.

**Not tested:** year 2 (runs are 1 year), sounds (E4), hornet adaptation (E2). The analysis is a script that reads the run directories alone (`experiments/analysis/e5_results.py`), written after this commit and before looking at any E5 result (while the runs go, only liveness, errors and spend are checked); any change of plan after the runs start is listed as an amendment, dated, and marked as made after the runs.

## Experiment 1: learning and culture (E1)

### Hypothesis

Pre-registered: AI bees with memory and hive talk (`full`) gather more than bees without talk (`no_talk`), without memory (`no_memory`), and the scripted rule and random bees. Primary measure: total deliveries per 2-year run. Supported if `full` is higher than each comparison arm consistently (same sign on every paired seed, and mean paired difference larger than its sd).

### Experimental setup

The run directories in "Main runs: shared setup". Every arm runs on the same seeds, so seed n has the same maze, bloom sites and first hornets in every arm, and arms are compared seed by seed. Seeds 0 to 2 for the pre-registered verdicts; seeds 0 to 4 for `full`, `no_talk`, `rule_bees` and `random_bees` (amendment 1 and the pre-registered baseline seeds).

### Variables or parameters

- **Changed:** the bee mind. `full` (memory and talk), `no_talk` (no `say`, nothing heard at home), `no_memory` (no episodes, notes, recall or remembered places), `rule_bees` (explore at random, take bloom when seen, go home at half life, no talk), `random_bees` (random intents).
- **Fixed:** the world and every parameter in the shared setup; the model and its settings in the AI arms.
- **Measured:** deliveries (primary); trips, stings, delivery rate, median ticks to bloom, stings per trip, mean colony size (secondary).

### Observations

- **Deliveries are rare in every arm:** 0 to 6 per 2-year run. Most come in the first summer (experiments/analysis/output/fig_deliveries_by_season.png).

- **What the bees say.** In `full`, the coordinates in hive talk point most often at home, and next at moving things. Of 1,621 coordinate pairs said at home (5 seeds), 585 point within 3 cells of a moving thing the speaker had seen, 111 near a current or recent bloom, and 925 at neither; 892 of those 925 point at home itself, within 4 cells of the hive centre (exploratory split). ## Experiment 2: arms race (E2)

### Hypothesis

Pre-registered: evolving hornets adapt to the AI colony, so stings per trip rise from year 1 to year 2, more than with `frozen_hornets`. Primary measure: stings per trip in year 2 minus year 1, over the three seasons with hornets in each year (stings / trips ended, from metrics.jsonl). Supported if the change is larger under `full`, consistently. Gene drift without a change in stings counts as drift, not adaptation.

### Experimental setup

`full` (seeds 0 to 4) against `frozen_hornets` (seeds 0 to 2), paired by seed 0 to 2; the other arms reported for reference. Under `frozen_hornets` the hornets keep the seed's initial draw of 8 genomes in every season and year; everything else, the AI bees included, is as in `full`.

### Variables or parameters

- **Changed:** hornet evolution (steady-state selection every 25 ticks, spring bred from the fittest autumn hornets) on or off.
- **Measured:** stings per trip by year (primary); hornet gene means at the end of each season with hornets; life removed by stings per bee-tick spent outside the hive.

### Observations

- **Stings per trip rose from year 1 to year 2 in most arms, evolving or not:** `full` 0.537 to 0.852, `frozen_hornets` 0.495 to 0.652, rule bees 0.632 to 0.824, random bees 0.140 to 0.295. `no_talk` fell, 0.563 to 0.490.
- **Genes drift, with no common direction.** Mean smell in the first and last season with hornets, `full` seeds 0 to 4: 0.5 to 0.125, 1.0 to 0.25, 4.0 to 4.25, 6.0 to 7.125, 3.5 to 4.375. Each seed's hornets settle on their own genome early and stay near it (experiments/analysis/output/fig_hornet_genes.png). Frozen hornets' means differ between seasons only because 4, 6 and then 8 of the same 8 genomes are on the map.
- **Selection has little to act on.** A `full` run has 17.2 stings in 2 years (sd 6.686, n 5), shared among 4 to 8 hornets.
- Life removed per bee-tick outside the hive: `full` 0.392 in year 1 (sd 0.219) and 0.392 in year 2 (sd 0.252); `frozen_hornets` 0.331 (sd 0.115) and 0.524 (sd 0.420).

### Results

| Arm | Year 1 | Year 2 | Change, mean (sd, n) | Change per seed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `full` | 0.537 (sd 0.284) | 0.852 (sd 0.559) | 0.315 (sd 0.516, n 5) | -0.0083, -0.4359, 0.7583, 0.65, 0.6111 |
| `frozen_hornets` | 0.495 (sd 0.289) | 0.652 (sd 0.325) | 0.158 (sd 0.497, n 3) | 0.72, -0.0238, -0.2235 |
| `rule_bees` | 0.632 (sd 0.257) | 0.824 (sd 0.226) | 0.192 (sd 0.311, n 5) | 0.2811, 0.0104, -0.0815, 0.6936, 0.056 |

Paired, `full` minus `frozen_hornets`, seeds 0 to 2: -0.7283, -0.4121, 0.9819; mean -0.0529, sd 0.9099: **no consistent difference.**

**Verdict: E2 is not supported.** The hornets' genes drifted, but stings did not rise more with evolution than without it, so by the pre-registered rule this is drift, not adaptation.

### Controls

- **Ablation:** `frozen_hornets` removes evolution; the year-2 rise is there without it.
- **Baseline:** rule and random bees show the same rise with evolving hornets, so it is not a response to anything the AI colony does.
- **Randomness:** the per-seed changes in `full` range from -0.4359 to 0.7583, wider than the mean difference between arms.

### What we learned

Stings per trip rise in year 2 for a reason other than evolution, one that holds even for frozen hornets and scripted bees; the arms race we designed for does not show at this scale. The likely reason is the weak selection signal: about 17 stings per run spread over up to 8 hornets cannot separate good genomes from lucky ones, so each seed's hornets drift to a different genome early on. This is an interpretation, not a measured result.

### What we would investigate next

Log each hornet's fitness at every replacement, to measure the selection differential directly; run longer (10 years) or with more bees per hornet so selection has more events; check what drives the year-2 rise in every arm (colony size, trip length, where hornets wait).

## Experiment 3: seasonal memory (E3)

### Hypothesis

Pre-registered: the colony finds the spring bloom sooner in year 2 than in year 1, because knowledge outlives the bees that found it; with hive talk switched off it does not. Primary measure: first spring delivery in year 1 minus year 2, in ticks into spring, censored at 250 (no delivery). Supported if positive under `full` and larger than under `no_talk`, consistently. Also reported: whether any bee lived through both springs, and whether the first year-2 spring forager had heard a coordinate within 3 cells of the spring bloom at home before leaving.

### Experimental setup

`full` against `no_talk`, paired by seed 0 to 2 (pre-registered) and 0 to 4 (amendment 1). The other arms reported for reference.

### Variables or parameters

- **Changed:** hive talk on or off.
- **Measured:** first spring delivery in each year (from metrics.jsonl), their difference, and the two carryover checks (from state.jsonl.gz, the say and trip events).

### Observations

- **Spring deliveries are rare.** `full` delivered in the first spring in 1 of 5 seeds (seed 0, tick 183) and in the second spring in 1 of 5 (seed 0, tick 83). `no_talk`: none in the first spring, one in the second (seed 4, tick 239).
- **No bee lived through both springs** in any seed of `full` or `no_talk`. In all 19 AI runs, one bee did (bee 3, `labels_good_bad` seed 0).
- **Every first delivery of the second spring in an AI arm came from a bee that had set out in winter.** There were 4 (`full` seed 0, `no_talk` seed 4, `no_memory` seed 2, `labels_good_bad` seed 2). The foragers left home at ticks 806, 895, 882 and 881 (winter is ticks 750 to 999), none was alive in the first spring, and none had heard a coordinate near the spring bloom at home.
- **One such bee, followed** (`full` seed 0, bee 9; the README still is from this trip). Hatched by the queen at tick 799, it left at tick 806 and explored east through the winter, its life falling from 495 to 109 by tick 999. It was turning for home when the spring bloom opened beside it: "At (39,30) exploring NW toward home; life 115" (tick 997), then "Bright cells found near (41,29); investigating them before heading home" (tick 1000). It carried the bloom home past the moving things it saw from tick 1053 on, and delivered at tick 1083, its life doubling from 26 to 52; a new bee hatched the same tick.

### Results

| Arm | Year 1, mean (sd, n) | Year 2, mean (sd, n) | Gain, mean (sd, n) | Gain per seed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `full` | 236.6 (sd 30.0, n 5) | 216.6 (sd 74.7, n 5) | 20.0 (sd 44.7, n 5) | 100, 0, 0, 0, 0 |
| `no_talk` | 250.0 (sd 0.0, n 5) | 247.8 (sd 4.9, n 5) | 2.2 (sd 4.9, n 5) | 0, 0, 0, 0, 11 |

Paired, `full` minus `no_talk`: seeds 0 to 2, 100, 0, 0 (mean 33.3333, sd 57.735); seeds 0 to 4, 100, 0, 0, 0, -11 (mean 17.8, sd 46.1974). **No consistent difference** either way. (experiments/analysis/output/fig_first_spring_delivery.png)

**Verdict: E3 is not supported.** The one positive gain under `full` came from a bee that happened to be near the bloom when it opened, not from anything passed on at home.

### Controls

- **Ablation:** `no_talk`, the switch that removes culture.
- **Culture against individual memory:** the two carryover checks were designed for exactly this case, and in every case they rule culture out.
- **Censoring:** most seeds have no spring delivery in either year, so their gain is 0 by construction (250 minus 250). The measure cannot tell "no change" from "no data".

### What we learned

Knowledge did not cross the year. A colony of about 3 bees, none living from one spring to the next, has almost no one to pass a place on to, and the measure we chose is dominated by where a bee happens to be when the bloom opens. The winter foragers are a surprise: the bees are never told about seasons, and some of them set out in the cold and were in the right place at the turn of the year.

### What we would investigate next

Measure transmission directly: was a spring-bloom coordinate said at home in year 2 before the first spring trip, and by whom? Use an uncensored measure (ticks to the first bloom sighting rather than the first delivery); test a world where bees can live a full year.

## Experiment 4: signals (E4)

### Hypothesis

Pre-registered: with neutral sounds, the sounds come to be tied to context (made near moving things, or near bright cells); with the names "good noise" and "bad noise" the tie is there from the start, because it comes from the words. Primary measure: mutual information between which sound a bee makes and its context at that decision (moving thing in view, bright cell in view, both, neither), with a permutation test (1,000 shuffles of the sound labels within each run). Supported if MI in `full` is above the 95th percentile of its permutation distribution in at least 2 of 3 seeds.

### Experimental setup

Sound-making decisions in every AI arm, from the `sound` and `ctx` fields of decision events. `full` and the other neutral arms against `labels_good_bad`, seeds 0 to 2 (and 3, 4 for `full`). The world treats both sounds the same in every arm: each costs 1 life and can draw hornets with a high hearing gene. Only the names in the prompt differ.

### Variables or parameters

- **Changed:** the sound names in the prompt (`sound-1`, `sound-2` against "good noise", "bad noise"). The world, the logs and the analysis use the neutral names in every arm.
- **Measured:** sounds made, MI and its permutation percentile, the listener response (share of `flee` or `go_home` at decisions triggered by hearing a sound, against timer decisions).

### Observations

- **Neutral sounds were almost never made:** 3 in 6,610 `full` decisions over 5 seeds, and none in 15,141 decisions of `no_talk`, `no_memory` and `frozen_hornets`. All 3 were `sound-1`, made by a bee carrying bloom home or just arrived, and its notes say why: "heading home W to report, alerting others" (seed 0, tick 124), "continuing home, sounding al[ert]" (seed 4, tick 326).
- **With the words, sounds were used more, and as the words suggest:** 9 in 4,342 `labels_good_bad` decisions, against 3 in 6,610 for `full`. 8 were "good noise", all made at home: 7 right after a delivery ("Delivered valuable thing home; life 645", seed 0, tick 178), and 1 in answer to another bee's ("Heard good noise from NW", seed 1, tick 447). The one "bad noise" came the moment a bee was stung while carrying: "Life dropped suddenly at (33,15) from moving thing at (33,14); fleeing south" (seed 2, tick 1026).
- Listeners: 6 decisions in `full` and 13 in `labels_good_bad` were triggered by a sound, too few to compare with the timer base rate.

### Results

| Arm | Sounds per seed | MI (bits) |
|---|---|---|
| `full` | 1, 0, 0, 0, 2 | missing in seeds 0 to 3 (fewer than 2 sounds); 0 in seed 4 (both `sound-1`, so MI is 0 by construction) |
| `no_talk`, `no_memory`, `frozen_hornets` | 0 in every seed | missing |
| `labels_good_bad` | 4, 3, 2 | 0 in seeds 0 and 1 (one sound only); 1.0 in seed 2 from 2 sounds, where every shuffle gives the same value (permutation p 1.0) |

**Verdict: E4 is not supported, and the primary test could not be run.** MI in `full` was above its permutation 95th percentile in 0 of 3 seeds, because there was nothing to measure. This is the pre-registered negative result: neutral sounds stayed meaningless.

### Controls

- **Prompt effect:** `labels_good_bad` is the control, and it shows the effect: the only sound use with a clear meaning is the one the words supply.
- **Permutation test:** planned and implemented (experiments/analysis/measures.py, `mi_test`, tested in tests/test_analysis.py), but with 0 to 4 sounds per run it has no power.
- **Hardcoding audit:** no code ties a sound to a context (see "Hardcoding audit").

### What we learned

Two sounds with a cost and no name are not worth using to these minds: the model weighs 1 life against nothing it can see. Given names, the same model uses them at once, and in the sense of the names. That is a prompt effect, which is exactly what this arm was built to catch, so in this system any meaning a sound has comes from its label. The pre-registered contexts also missed where sounds were made: almost all came at home after a delivery, which the four context classes put under "neither".

### What we would investigate next

A free-sound arm, pre-registered before it runs, to tell "meaningless" apart from "too expensive"; contexts that include home and carrying; listener effects measured on what bees do in the 10 ticks after hearing a sound.

## Why trips failed (diagnosis after the main runs, not a result)

Written 2026-11-06 after the main results, to choose the world for E5. It is a description of the main runs, not a test of anything. Generated by `experiments/analysis/trip_diagnosis.py` from the run directories alone (output: experiments/analysis/output/trip_diagnosis.md). A trip counts in the season it started; distances are path steps from home; "share of the way" is a trip's farthest point divided by the path distance to that season's bloom.

Spring trips, seeds 0 to 4, pooled:

| Arm | Trips | Delivered | Died, stung | Died, not stung | Home, stung | Home, not stung, empty | Reached bloom | Median share of the way | Median life at turn-back (share of L) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `full` | 68 | 8 | 12 | 4 | 10 | 34 | 10 | 0.658 | 0.807 |
| `no_talk` | 55 | 6 | 11 | 4 | 5 | 29 | 11 | 0.914 | 0.707 |
| `rule_bees` | 90 | 5 | 7 | 6 | 33 | 39 | 7 | 0.504 | 0.647 |

- **The bottleneck is finding the bloom, not carrying it home.** Of the `full` spring trips that reached the bloom, 8 of 10 delivered. Half of all `full` spring trips (34 of 68) came home unstung and empty.
- **AI bees turn back early with most of their life left.** `full` bees that came home empty turned at a median 0.52 of the way to the bloom with 0.807 L of life (median over those trips). Life is not what ends these trips.

## Experiment 5: talk with something to carry (E5, post hoc)

A follow-up designed after the main results and because of them; it does not change the verdicts of E1 to E4. Pre-registered in "Pre-registration", "E5", committed as 0056c1a before any E5 AI run. ### Hypothesis

Pre-registered: in a world where the bloom is reachable, (a) `e5_full` delivers more than `e5_no_talk`, paired by seed (same sign on all 5 seeds and mean paired difference larger than its sd); and (b) some `e5_full` deliveries are talk-informed: the bee heard at home a coordinate within 3 cells of that season's bloom before it first came within sight of the bloom (supported if this happens in at least 2 of 5 seeds).

- **World:** the "E5 world" (Setup calibration): the main world at scale 0.7, 56 x 34, bloom from 38 steps, 2, 3 and 4 hornets in spring, summer and autumn, 1 year. Chosen with scripted bees only, to a target fixed before the runs.
- **Runs:** `e5_full` and `e5_no_talk`, seeds 0 to 4, one process per seed, from a clean worktree at 0056c1a, all `git_dirty: false`. Run directories `experiments/results/e5_full/20261106T112913Z_s<n>/seed-<n>` and the same under `e5_no_talk`.

- **Changed:** hive talk on (`e5_full`) or off (`e5_no_talk`).
- **Fixed:** the E5 world, the model and its settings, the seeds.

It explored NE, and every note after that names the spot near (37,3) as where it is going ("bright spot near (37,3) is the target", tick 177). It came within sight of the bloom at tick 216 and delivered at tick 330. Bee 7, told by bee 3 at tick 170 ("Bright spot seen near (37,3)"), went the same way, though its notes say the spot was "already taken"; it delivered at tick 332.
- **The pre-registered acted-on-talk check missed bee 6.** It counts only `go_to` toward the bloom. Bee 6 steered with `explore NE` and kept the coordinate in its notes, so the check scores 0. This was found by reading the notes after the results and is not counted.

### Controls

- **Ablation:** `e5_no_talk` removes talk and nothing else, on the same seeds; it delivered no less.
- **Is the world winnable, and not trivial?** Scripted bees in the same world, before any AI run: oracle 0.889 of spring trips, rule 0.210, random 0.000 (Setup calibration, "E5 world").
- **Chance in the talk-informed measure:** a warning about moving things near the bloom would also hit the 3-cell rule. Both talk-informed deliveries came from plain bloom reports by bees that had seen the bloom ("speaker had seen the bloom: True"), not from such coincidences.
- **Hardcoding audit:** the prompts and the mind are unchanged since the main runs, where the audit and the leak and no-hints tests passed; 67 tests pass at this commit.
- **Replay:** all 10 E5 runs replay identically offline from their packed caches (experiments/analysis/output/replay_e5.log), 9.0 to 19.5 s each.
- **Not run:** prompt robustness, other world scales, more seeds.

### What we learned

Making the bloom reachable gave the colony something to say, and in one seed talk did carry a place: two newborns heard where the bloom was, headed that way, and brought it home. But talk did not pay. Colonies with talk took fewer trips (26.6 against 40.6) and spent more of their time at home (0.430 against 0.239, exploratory), and colonies without talk found the summer and autumn blooms sooner and delivered as much or more. Our reading, not a measured result: in this system talk costs foraging time. Bees wait at home to report, so the time spent talking cancels what the talk carries. The spread across seeds (1 to 18 deliveries per year) is far larger than any effect five seeds could show. We also learned that our acted-on-talk rule was too narrow: a bee can follow what it heard with `explore` in the right direction, not only with `go_to`.

### What we would investigate next

Pre-register a broader acted-on-talk measure (any move toward the heard place before the sighting); separate the time cost of talk from its content, for example with an arm in which bees hear talk without waiting at home for it; run 10 or more seeds; test whether talk pays where knowledge goes stale, with a bloom that moves within a season.

## Negative results

Each of these was pre-registered as a possible outcome and is reported in full in its experiment above.

1. **The AI mind does not out-gather a simple rule (E1).** Expected: `full` delivers more than `rule_bees`. Happened: 1.8 deliveries per 2 years each (sd 1.643 and 1.483, n 5), paired differences 3, 1, -1, -4, 1. How we know: 5 paired seeds, the pre-registered consistency rule. What it taught us: in this world the model's judgement buys safety (fewer trips, fewer stings per trip than rule bees, consistently) but not food, and colonies too small to keep anything.
2. **Memory and talk do not help gathering (E1).** Expected: removing either lowers deliveries. Happened: both ablations delivered at least as much on average (2.6 and 2.667 against 1.8), with no consistent difference.
3. **No arms race (E2).** Expected: evolving hornets raise stings per trip in year 2 more than frozen ones. Happened: stings rose in both, and in the scripted arms too; paired differences -0.7283, -0.4121, 0.9819. Genes drifted, so we report drift. It taught us that selection on about 17 stings per run is too weak to measure.
4. **No seasonal memory (E3).** Expected: an earlier spring delivery in year 2, through talk. Happened: 1 of 5 `full` seeds delivered in year-2 spring, by a bee that set out in winter and never heard the place; no bee lived through both springs. It taught us that the measure is dominated by where a bee is when the bloom opens.
5. **Neutral sounds stayed meaningless (E4).** Expected: sounds tied to context. Happened: 3 sounds in 6,610 decisions, so mutual information could not be estimated; with labels, sounds were used in the sense of the words. It taught us that in this system a sound's meaning comes from its name.
6. **Avoidance is not learned (further measures).** We had hoped to see bees learn that moving things hurt. Newborns flee them from the first sighting in every arm, without memory too, so it is not learned from experience. It comes from the model's prior, the wording, or both: round 1 offered a `flee` action, and round 2's H6 tests the wording without it. The phase 6 pilot with abstract words (one seed) saw none of 4 units move away at first sight.
7. **Talk did not pay, even with something to carry (E5, post hoc).** Expected: in a world where the bloom is reachable, `e5_full` delivers more than `e5_no_talk`, and bees in at least 2 of 5 seeds deliver from a place they heard at home. Happened: 5.2 against 8.2 deliveries per year (paired differences 12, 0, -14, -6, -7), and talk-informed deliveries in 1 of 5 seeds (2 deliveries). How we know: 5 paired seeds, the pre-registered rules. What it taught us: talk can carry a place (seed 0), but bees with talk spent more time at home (0.430 of bee-ticks against 0.239, exploratory) and took fewer trips, so in this system the cost of talking cancels what it carries.
8. **No discovery without being told (round 2, H10).** Expected: in at least 3 of 5 `r2_b_full` seeds, a unit that delivered once chooses go_to_O with flag F again. Happened: 0 of 5 seeds, over 3 completed years each; 9 deliveries in all (7 deliberate), and none of those units chose it again. How we know: the pre-registered repeat-choice measure on every decision, with random bees (0 deliveries in 8 years) and rule bees (repeat choices in every seed) as bounds. What it taught us: the one event "your counter C rose" was not enough for the model to link delivery to its reward; the told arm (A_full, one seed) made 59 repeat choices (3 units).
9. **Talk did not spread delivery (round 2, H7).** Expected: talk-informed first deliberate deliveries in B_full above first deliberate deliveries in B_no_talk in 4 of 5 seeds. Happened: 0 of 5 (1, 0, 0, 0, 0 against 2, 1, 9, 3, 3), with hundreds of broadcasts about G or O. What it taught us: as in E5, units with talk delivered less on average, not more (0.6 against 1.6667 per year; not a pre-registered test).
10. **No seasonal memory through talk (round 2, H8).** Expected: an earlier first G reach each spring after year 1 in B_full in 4 of 5 pairs. Happened: 3 of 5. What it taught us: as in E3, where units are when spring opens dominates this measure.

## Limits

- **n = 3 for three AI arms and n = 5 for two.** With these spreads only large, consistent differences can be seen; a real effect of one delivery per run would be missed.
- **One world size, one difficulty.** Setup sensitivity (world size, timing, population) was not tested for the AI arms. The calibration varied three knobs with scripted bees only.
- **No prompt-robustness arm.** The spec's paraphrased-prompt stretch was not run, so the AI arms' behaviour may depend on the prompt's wording; `labels_good_bad` shows the words matter for sounds.
- **One model at one setting** (`claude-haiku-5-5`, effort low, thinking off). A stronger model might use memory and talk differently.
- **The pre-registered sound contexts** (moving thing or bright cell in view) did not include home or carrying, where most sounds were made.
- **E3's measure is censored:** most seeds have no spring delivery in either year.
- **E5 is post hoc** and was run in one world (scale 0.7), for 1 year, on 5 seeds, with the same prompt. Its world was chosen after the main results, with scripted bees only. Deliveries ranged from 1 to 18 per run, so an effect of a few deliveries would be missed.

### Hypothesis

- **H10 (discovery, the headline).** In B arms, which are never told what delivery does, units discover it: in at least 3 of 5 `r2_b_full` seeds, some unit makes a *repeat choice* (defined below) within the completed years.
- **H7 (culture).** Units that never delivered themselves make deliberate deliveries after hearing a broadcast about G or O. Paired by seed, `r2_b_full`'s talk-informed first deliberate deliveries outnumber `r2_b_no_talk`'s first deliberate deliveries in at least 4 of 5 seeds.
- **H8 (seasonal memory).** Paired by seed, the first G reach each spring after year 1 comes earlier in `r2_b_full` than in `r2_b_no_talk`, in at least 4 of 5 seeds.
- **H6 (labels and prior).** At the first sighting of K, before any halving, units move away or go to O less often in `r2_b_full` (abstract words) than in `r2_n_full` (round 1 neutral words).
- **H11 (does knowing the rule matter).** `r2_a_full` (told the rule) against `r2_b_full` on deliveries per completed year.
- **H9 (exploratory, no decision rule).** Signal use over time.

### Measures and decision rules

All from `events.jsonl` (and `state.jsonl.gz` for positions), computed by `experiments/analysis/round2.py`, committed before any round 2 result exists. Names below are the sim's: `go_home` is the abstract `go_to_O`, `carrying` is flag F, `bloom` is a G pickup, `wait` is `hold`.

- **GOF choice:** a `decision` event with intent `go_home` taken while flag F was set (`ctx.carrying` = 1; for scripted runs, which log no ctx, the unit's `carrying` field in the state line of the tick before).
- **Deliberate delivery (DD):** a `delivery` event whose unit's most recent decision at or before that tick was a GOF choice.
- **Repeat choice (H10):** a GOF choice by a unit at tick t2 when that same unit has a `delivery` event at some t1 < t2. A seed shows discovery if any unit makes one within the completed years. Supported if 3 or more of 5 B_full seeds show it. Also reported: the count per seed, how many of those repeat choices ended in a delivery, the same for B_no_talk, A_full, N_full and the scripted arms.
- **Broadcast about G or O:** a `say` event whose text contains `G` or `O` as a whole word (regex `\b[GO]\b`, case-sensitive).
- **First DD:** a DD by a unit with no earlier `delivery` event. **Talk-informed first DD:** a first DD whose unit is in `heard_by` of a broadcast about G or O at an earlier tick. H7: per seed, talk-informed first DDs in B_full (X) against first DDs in B_no_talk (Y), within the pair's completed years. Supported if X > Y in at least 4 of 5 seeds. Also reported: all first DDs in both arms, and B_full's first DDs that were not talk-informed.
- **First G reach in a spring (H8):** for each year y >= 2 completed by both runs of a pair, the ticks from the start of that spring to its first `bloom` event; a spring with none counts as 250 (censored, and reported as such). Per run, the mean over those years. Supported if B_full's mean is lower than B_no_talk's in at least 4 of 5 seeds. Not testable for a pair that completes fewer than 2 years.
- **First sighting (H6):** `experiments/analysis/first_sighting.py`: each unit's first decision triggered by a new K entity in view, before that unit's first halving, classified away, toward, hold or zone O (`away_or_zone_O_share`), within the years completed by every B_full and N_full run. Supported if the B_full per-seed mean share is lower than N_full's and the two arms' per-seed ranges do not overlap; "direction only" if the means differ that way but the ranges overlap; otherwise not supported. The pooled counts are reported too.
- **Deliveries per completed year (H11):** `delivery` events in the completed years divided by their number. "Knowing raises deliveries" if A_full exceeds B_full in all 3 paired seeds (0 to 2); "knowing lowers" if below in all 3; otherwise no difference shown. Means, sd and n are reported for all 5 B_full seeds as well.
- **Signals (H9):** per season, decisions with a signal per unit-decision, and the share of those made with a K entity in view (`ctx.movers` > 0) or with flag F set. Descriptive only.

### Controls

- **Talk ablation:** B_no_talk (H7, H8). **Baseline:** scripted rule and random bees in the same world (what DDs look like when hardcoded, and by chance). **Prompt:** A_full against B_full is the one-sentence prompt change; N_full against B_full is the word change (H6). **Hardcoding audit:** the B prompt never says what flag F or zone O does (tests/test_vocab.py checks it word by word); the only link a B unit gets is the event "your counter C rose" after a delivery. **Setup:** E5 world, one world size only; this is a limit of round 2.

### Controls

- **Talk ablation (B_no_talk):** delivered more than B_full on average (1.6667 against 0.6 per year, not a pre-registered test), and was the only B arm with repeat choices, so talk is not what produced them.
- **Baselines:** scripted rule bees make DDs and repeat choices in every seed (hardcoded); random bees make no delivery in 8 years in any seed, so a delivery in this world does not happen by chance.
- **Prompt:** A_full (told the rule) and N_full (neutral words) both delivered more than any B_full seed, on one seed each.
- **Hardcoding audit:** the B prompt never says what flag F or zone O does (tests/test_vocab.py).
- **Setup:** one world size; amendment 1 cut A_full and N_full to one seed each.

### What we learned

Units that are never told what delivery does reach zone O with flag F, see counter C double, and do not go back for more. In 5 seeds and 3 years each, no B_full unit repeated the choice, and talk did not spread it. The one-sentence rule (A_full) or familiar words (N_full) gave many repeat choices on one seed each. With abstract words the model treats K as harmless at first sight (0.1472 away or to O) where with neutral words it avoids it (0.8929, one seed only), in line with round 1's reading that first-sight avoidance comes from the words or the model's prior rather than from experience.

### What we would investigate next

Run A_full and N_full on 5 seeds to turn H6 and H11 into tests; give B units the reward event with more salience (or a memory note of it) and test whether one link is enough for discovery; check why B_no_talk units repeated the choice and B_full units did not.
